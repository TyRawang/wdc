Container Land

Container Land rents, sells and customizes shipping containers, and offers mobile storage and self-storage across Calgary and southern Alberta.

The project

We built a conversion-focused website that takes customers from browsing to booking in a few clicks. The homepage explains the process in four simple steps, and dedicated pages for every service and every town they serve help the business show up in local searches.

Highlights

  • Online booking: pick a container size, date and location
  • Separate pages for renting, buying, container buildings, mobile storage, self-storage and freight shipping
  • Location pages for 21 communities, from Airdrie to Vulcan
  • Verified Google reviews shown on the homepage
  • Click-to-call and free-quote buttons always in the header
