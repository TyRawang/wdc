Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Web Design Calgary ("we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

Information we collect

We only collect personal information that you choose to give us, such as when you fill out our contact form or request a free audit. This may include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your website address and business details
  • Anything you include in your message, such as project goals

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also record basic technical information, such as your browser type and IP address, to keep the site secure and working properly.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry or audit request
  • To prepare proposals and deliver services you ask for
  • To keep the website secure and improve how it works

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We will not add you to a mailing list without your permission.

Third-party services

We use a small number of trusted services to run this website, which may process limited information on our behalf:

  • Google Fonts to display the fonts on this site, which means your browser connects to Google's servers
  • Our website host and any form service used to deliver contact and audit form messages to us

If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this policy before doing so. Some of these providers may store information outside Canada, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Third-party services listed above may set their own cookies as described in their privacy policies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond to you, provide our services and meet legal or accounting requirements. After that, we securely delete it.

How we protect information

We use reasonable physical, technical and administrative safeguards to protect your information. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, but we take care to limit who can access it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits. To make a request, please contact us.

Links to other websites

Our site links to other websites, including client sites in our portfolio. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites, so please review their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your information, please get in touch.
